Describing an issue

These details can help explain what happened:

  • Your device model and iOS version.
  • The version of Injury Report you’re using.
  • What you were trying to do, what happened, and what you expected.
  • A screenshot, if helpful, with personal or sensitive information removed.

Do not include passwords or other sensitive account information.
